Factory Tour

Give us 48 hours.
Eat all the ice cream you want.

We give pre-arranged tours to school students, colleges and youth groups — a genuinely good way to learn how ice cream, bakery and dairy actually get made.

How it works

Book your Frubon Factory Tour

  1. Send us your group size and preferred date — at least 48 hours ahead.
  2. We confirm a slot at the Mahindra World City plant, Jaipur.
  3. Walk the churn hall, the bakery and the dairy dock with our team.
  4. Finish in the tasting room. There is no scoop limit.
