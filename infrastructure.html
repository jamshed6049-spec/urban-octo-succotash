The plant

Built for the product, and for the planet

Spread across an area of over 60,000 sq. ft., the manufacturing plant is one of the most modern plants of Northern India, equipped with the latest testing and manufacturing facilities.

Special care has been taken for recycling and re-usage of water. The water recycled using ETP is utilized for cleaning & gardening. Provision for ample ventilation and fresh light has been kept to reduce the carbon footprint and dependency on electricity during day time.

It is our continuous endeavour to create value for one of our most important stake holders — the environment.
