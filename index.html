Why Frubon

Value without compromise.
Everytime, everywhere.

Milk twice a day

Collected from our own village farmer societies and chilled at source — morning and evening, every day.

Real milk fat. Never vegetable oil.

Frubon makes ice cream, not frozen dessert. It is a harder, costlier way to do it. It is the only way we do it.

100% vegetarian

No eggs. No animal fat. Nothing in a Frubon pack that everyone at the table cannot share.

Tested at every stage

Raw material, semi-finished and finished goods — including microbiological testing on every batch.
