The standard

Ice cream, or
frozen dessert.

Most of what is sold in India as ice cream is frozen dessert — made with vegetable oil because it is cheaper and easier to freeze.

Frubon is made with fresh cream and milk fat. It costs more, it is harder to run, and it is the whole reason the brand exists. We have never manufactured frozen dessert and we do not intend to start.

Our own collection

Milk from Dev Milk Foods’ village farmer societies, chilled at source.

100% vegetarian

No eggs, no animal fat — in anything we have ever made.

Inside the plant

The part of the brand
nobody photographs

A lab that can stop the line, a team that keeps the discipline, and a building designed to give some of what it takes back.

Quality

An in-house lab, not an outsourced certificate

Microbiological and physical testing run on site, across raw material, semi-finished and finished goods.

People

The same hands, every shift

Whites, hairnets and a trained line team — the hygiene discipline is the product, not paperwork around it.

Plant

60,000 sq ft, built to be walked through

One of the most modern dairy, ice cream and bakery setups in Northern India — and open to visitors on 48 hours’ notice.

Environment

Water that goes round again

Water recycled through the ETP runs the cleaning and the gardens; daylight and ventilation cut the load on the grid.
